    How it works

    From first conversation to first touch.

    A simple, guided journey from your first enquiry to a home that's ready to use.

    1. 1
      Step 01

      Consultation & requirements

      Tell us about your home, your routines and what you would like to automate. We talk through switches, lighting, curtains, locks and cameras that suit your space.

    2. 2
      Step 02

      Smart-home solution planning

      We plan a room-by-room solution and help you choose between retrofit and fully integrated automation, depending on your home and wiring.

    3. 3
      Step 03

      Installation & configuration

      The products are installed and programmed, with scenes, switches and controls set up the way you want to use them.

    4. 4
      Step 04

      Demonstration & handover

      We walk you through every control so the whole household feels comfortable with their smart home from day one.

    A typical journey. Exact steps are tailored to each home and project.
